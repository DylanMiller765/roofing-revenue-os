## Commercial judgment

A dashboard alone is not an acquisition offer. Keep the pilot terms close to the owner demo, but do not build another generic agency homepage. The current $0 first-month management offer should be exchanged for response ownership, sufficient agreed media budget and disciplined outcome updates. Free work without those commitments cannot produce a useful case study.

No present evidence proves ad-account performance, lead delivery, real attribution or signed revenue. Local mock testing cannot certify a launch.

## Design contract

Palette: ink #142C41, blue #245BA8, teal #23776B, mist #F3F6F9, line #DCE3EA, white #FFFFFF. Amber is reserved for review states. Manrope variable is self-hosted; 400/500 for body, 600/700 for interface, 600 for spacious headings. Tabular figures for economics.

Funnel: left-aligned homeowner message, a compact form beside it, and a restrained architectural roof diagram supporting the subject. On phones the CTA leads directly to the form; explanatory process content follows intake. The roof drawing is an illustration, never job proof.

Operator: persistent desktop navigation, lightweight phone navigation, acquisition KPIs above a cumulative outcome path and a concrete booking bottleneck. Campaigns and recommendations use progressive disclosure. Color signals outcomes and risk, not decoration.

Review against the brief: reject a generic agency hero with fake social proof, eight interchangeable KPI boxes, decorative growth sparklines, and an AI chat interface. The distinctive element is the connection between acquisition cost and each sales stage. No invented historical trend is necessary.

## Live-client launch gaps deliberately left visible

The schema is a design sketch, not a deployed CRM: UTM columns, authenticated access/row-level policies, durable intake, consent records, deduplication/spam detection, source-to-outcome joins and provider reconciliation still need implementation and authorized testing. The current qualification function accepts duplicate/spam context, but the API does not detect either. A syntactically valid phone number is not evidence of reachability. The UI now says so.

A production mutation adapter also needs durable, exact-proposal approvals and audit storage, concurrent budget-window enforcement and fresh execution-time data. The local policy lab is educational and never calls the executor. Do not use its synthetic high-risk scenario as evidence for a geographic expansion.
