# Founding Client Offer v1

## Positioning
We build and operate a Google customer-acquisition system for one Houston roofing company as our founding case study.

## Offer
- $0 setup fee for the founding client.
- $0 management fee for the first 30 days.
- Client funds Google Ads directly.
- We build the landing page, campaign structure, call/form tracking, lead-journey tracking and daily optimization workflow.
- Client keeps ownership of their ad account and business data.
- After 30 days, continuation is optional and priced based on scope/results.
- In exchange for the free pilot, client agrees that anonymized or approved results may be used in a case study/testimonial if they are satisfied.

## Required client commitments
- Minimum test budget agreed before launch; do not promise a lead quantity until auction data and budget are known.
- Answer/return new calls and leads quickly.
- Provide lead outcomes (qualified, booked, estimate, won/lost) so optimization can use sales quality.
- Do not materially change the funnel/campaigns without telling us during the pilot.
- Use only accurate claims in ads/landing pages.

## Success metrics, in order
1. Qualified lead rate
2. Cost per qualified lead
3. Booked-inspection rate
4. Cost per booked inspection
5. Estimates generated
6. Converted leads / signed jobs
7. Attributed revenue when reliable
