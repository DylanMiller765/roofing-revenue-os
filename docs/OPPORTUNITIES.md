## Evidence checked 8 September 2026

Google describes [qualified and converted leads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11459091) as stages defined by an advertiser's offline lead process. This supports the measurement direction; it does not establish that this demo has a working integration.

Google's [landing-page reporting guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7543502) supports evaluating destination and mobile experience. Message match and lower friction remain hypotheses to validate against qualified and booked outcomes, not only form conversion.

Google's current [conversion category documentation](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/conversions/categories) directs new offline-outcome integrations toward Data Manager and notes a June 15, 2026 restriction on new UploadClickConversion usage. Reverify the exact account/token/API eligibility at implementation time.
