# V1 service and demo script

## Explain it to a roofer
“Pick something a homeowner might search. Someone looking for a leak repair sees a page about that problem. Someone considering a new roof sees the replacement page. We write the message and make contacting you easy. Your existing website and sales process can stay as they are.”

“The homeowner calls or fills out a short form, and the lead goes straight to your chosen phone or inbox. On our side, we track spend, source, roofing need and whether the lead fits your area and qualification criteria. We review weak search terms and qualified-lead cost, then optimize the ads within spending and approval safeguards.”

The on-screen demo is fictional. It does not deliver leads, place calls, connect an account or change ads.

## How the walkthrough works
- `/` starts with the replacement example. Previous/next controls, hero dragging or touch swipes select `intent=replacement`, `storm` or `repair` and update the headline, action, explanation and form concern together.
- Adding `preview=homeowner` hides the explanatory demo controls. This remains visibly fictional.
- Existing query attribution is preserved through example and preview links. The existing session capture remains in place for other navigation.
- Ads would use configured destination URLs by intent group. The demo does not discover an individual's raw Google search or use a self-learning conversion model. Do not create a page per keyword.
- Better relevance is the design hypothesis. Measure qualified-lead conversion and CPQL to learn what actually performs. Google's [setup guidance](https://business.google.com/us/resources/articles/how-to-setup-google-ads-a-checklist/) recommends landing pages relevant to ads and keywords; it does not promise a lift for our pages.

## Month one: client responsibilities
Provide or help set up Ads access; fund an agreed media budget directly; approve business facts, service area and claims; choose the phone/inbox receiving leads; name someone to respond promptly. No new CRM or mandatory sales-stage reporting.

## Measurement and boundaries
After live integrations are configured and tested: ad spend, campaign/search-term data, tracked inbound calls, form requests, source/intent and form qualification checks. Qualification is plausibility, not verified contactability. Calls require call evidence or a manual review; clicks on a phone link do not count as qualified callers. Real cross-channel reporting must deduplicate repeat contacts.

Today: 36 fictional leads = 13 calls + 23 forms, of which 24 are qualified in the mock cohort. Filtered campaign counts reconcile. Neither channel is live or delivered. Search waste is selected spend flagged for review, not guaranteed recoverable savings. Downstream examples are collapsed by default.

Optional/manual: reviewing disputed fit, call quality, spam, and any booked inspection, estimate or signed job the roofer chooses to share. Later: deeper CRM integration, trustworthy revenue attribution and offline sales uploads. Keep these architecture foundations without making the pilot depend on them.
