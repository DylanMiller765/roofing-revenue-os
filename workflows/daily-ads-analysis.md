# Workflow: Daily Ads Analysis

1. Pull yesterday + trailing 7/30 day Google Ads metrics.
2. Pull lead-stage outcomes for the same attributed leads.
3. Validate tracking completeness before interpreting performance.
4. Calculate CPL, CPQL, booked-inspection CPA and converted-lead CPA.
5. Identify search terms with meaningful spend and no qualified outcome.
6. Compare campaign quality—not just CPL.
7. Draft recommendations with evidence, confidence and expected upside/risk.
8. Save recommendations as PROPOSED.
9. Human reviews and approves/rejects.
10. Only an approved action may enter a future write executor.
